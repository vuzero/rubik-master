Paint your cube's colors

Pick a color, then click or drag across the stickers. Each face has 16 stickers; each color appears 16 times.

  1. 1Choose any side as Front and the side above it as Top. Keep that grip while you enter colors.
  2. 2Top: tilt the cube toward you; its bottom row touches Front. Bottom: tilt away; its top row touches Front.
  3. 3Left, Right, Back: turn the cube left or right, keeping Top up. Each face's top row is next to Top.
